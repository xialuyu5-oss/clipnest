ClipNest helps you choose a source format, review the duration and estimated size, and confirm each download. Follow progress and remaining-time estimates, pause when supported, or cancel to remove cached files.

![Quality selection with the original demo](../images/desktop-20260920.png)

Interface preview only. Try the project's original animation without installing anything. This preview cannot analyze real platform links and does not prove that a platform download works.

## Your device. Your files.

On PC, install and start the local component. The website checks its environment and opens the complete tool. Analysis, video transfer and merging run on your computer; the ClipNest website does not relay video traffic.

## One place, 19 source platforms

The source includes dedicated extractors for 19 platforms and recognizable official icons. The nine newest integrations have not all passed live-link acceptance. Availability depends on source permissions, region and platform changes.

## Mobile editions, with clear limits

Android has an on-device engine in a development preview; public APK distribution and physical-device acceptance are pending. The WeChat preview handles direct MP4 links only. An iOS app is not available.

Developed through OpenAI Vibe Coding, using ChatGPT and Codex. An independent project, not an official OpenAI product.

Only download content you own or are authorized to use. ClipNest does not remove DRM.
