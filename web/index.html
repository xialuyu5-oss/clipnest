A SMALL GUIDE

Save a video in three steps

1

Copy and analyze a link

Paste a single video's share link. Share text containing one link also works.

2

Choose a format and quality

MP4 is widely supported; WebM and MKV can preserve other codecs. Playback depends on the codec. Low-resolution videos are never upscaled to 4K.

3

Wait, then save to your device

Confirm your permission and download choice. When processing finishes, select Save file. Ready does not mean saved to your device.

Why is 1080p or 4K missing?

Only available source formats are shown. Access restrictions or missing server components may reduce the choices. Portrait quality uses the shorter edge.

Why can my browser play a link that fails here?

Browser login and network settings are not shared with the server. Check server connectivity and video access. Only Bilibili account login is supported.

Where are files saved, and for how long?

Files stay on this computer until you delete the task. Paused downloads survive a server restart. Keep this browser's site data to find your tasks again.

Privacy and permitted use

No ads, analytics or third-party frontend scripts. Analysis contacts source platforms; thumbnails may contact their CDN. Do not redistribute protected works without permission.
